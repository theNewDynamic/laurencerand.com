Lauren Cerand is the founder and owner of Lauren Cerand Public Relations, and has been active in the field since 2001. In 2025, **Morgan Jerkins** said, when [profiled](https://paw.princeton.edu/article/morgan-jerkins-14-explores-love-across-generations-new-novel) in *Princeton Alumni Weekly*, “I knew it would take a village—in-house with HarperCollins and with outside publicist Lauren Cerand—for [Zeal](https://bookshop.org/p/books/zeal-a-novel-morgan-jerkins/2e1e67f1c2931244?ean=9780063234093&next=t) to succeed.”

**Cerand is available for strategic consulting in 2026, and for book campaigns requested by new clients, she is reading for April 2027 and beyond publication dates.**

Her publicity list in 2026 includes:

Winter: [Generator](https://bookshop.org/p/books/generator-rinny-gremaud/5eb3b69455418e98?ean=9781639640713&next=t) by **Rinny Gremaud,** a debut novel, translated by **Holly James**, that *Shelf Awareness* says, “provides a deeply meditative examination of identity,” *Foreword Reviews* called “enthralling, genre-bending,” and *Publishers Weekly* says, “leaves readers with much to chew on,” (Schaffner Press, January). [Hostages: A Counter-History of Colonial Plunder](https://bookshop.org/p/books/hostages-taina-tervonen/04e0d16e0761f7ed?ean=9781639640621&next=t) by **Taina Tervonen**, new narrative nonfiction from [The Bone Whisperers](https://bookshop.org/p/books/the-bone-whisperers-two-women-scientists-and-their-work-to-connect-lost-lives-in-bosnia-herzegovina-taina-tervonen/4c204e7c62d712d7?ean=9781639640485&next=t) author and 2022 winner of the Jan Michalski Prize for Literature, translated by **Sara Hanaburgh** (Schaffner Press, February). Read an [interview](https://www.washingtonindependentreviewofbooks.com/features/an-interview-with-taina-tervonen) at the *Washington Independent Review of Books,* which notes, "Tervonen’s investigation follows the long effort to bring these objects home and finds in the story a wider reckoning with what colonial plunder truly costs."

Spring: [The Daughters,](https://bookshop.org/p/books/the-daughters-joanna-margaret/e637a9b605f6346e?ean=9781613166772&next=t) praised by *The Nerd Daily* as “pure atmosphere and that is what keeps the pages turning,” by "[historian-turned-novelist](https://andibartz.substack.com/p/guest-post-3-tips-for-nailing-historical)" **Joanna Margaret** (Mysterious Press, March), interviewed on WHMP's [*Writers' Block*](https://soundcloud.com/whmp/novelist-joanna-margaret-the-daughters-a-thriller-odyssey-bookshop-reading-wed-513-2?in=whmp/sets/author-author-1) *with Megan Zinn*, who said,"I love a book that makes the work of librarians exciting...librarians as heroes, particularly in a thriller"; and **Deborah Brown English’s** [Time’s Breath](https://bookshop.org/p/books/time-s-breath-an-odyssey-in-words-and-pictures-deborah-brown-english/537eea69ea667425?ean=9798998560002&next=t) art exhibition and debut illustrated novel, praised in *Bmore Art* as, “both a deconstruction and a reconstruction of the classic illustrated fantasy fiction books of the past.” In late March, Superbook, created by the **Italian Cultural Institute in New York**, kicked off with a four-day festival designed to bring together top Italian authors and American readers that *Publishers Weekly* deemed “[original](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/awards-and-prizes/article/99954-italian-authors-to-compete-for-new-superbook-award-in-america.html)," and will continue in September. In May, two newly reissued novels by **Howard Rodman**, [Destiny Express](https://bookshop.org/p/books/destiny-express-howard-a-rodman/1ba69aeb22996846?ean=9781644285534&next=t)*,* praised by *Alta* as, *"*Boldly imagined, full of glamour and peril, this expressionist novel considers what people sacrifice when history leaves them no good options," and [The Great Eastern](https://bookshop.org/p/books/the-great-eastern-howard-a-rodman/7518d73745f361ca?ean=9781644285541&next=t)*.*

Summer: **Boman Desai's** new novel, [Brahms Comes to Dinner](https://www.ipgbook.com/brahms-comes-to-dinner-products-9781639640836.php) (Schaffner Press, June), excerpted in print and [online](https://brooklynrail.org/2026/05/fiction/from-brahms-comes-to-dinner/) at the *Brooklyn Rail,* which notes, "By dramatizing the high-voltage, yet tender, minds of three legendary musicians, Boman Desai has written a *When We Cease to Understand the World* for nineteenth-Century classical music."

In 2027, confirmed authors include **Kirmen Uribe,** whose work has been translated into more than twenty languages, and will next appear in the U.S. with [Life Before Dolphins](https://bookshop.org/p/books/life-before-dolphins-kirmen-uribe/1fa758eebd65cec4?ean=9781566897730&next=t)*,* translated by **Megan McDowell** (Coffee House Press, January); and [Only the Lie is True](https://www.ipgbook.com/only-the-lie-is-true-products-9781639640935.php) by **Malik Sam**, translated by **Marjolijn de Jager** (Schaffner Press, January).

Cerand holds a Bachelor’s of Science degree in Industrial & Labor Relations from Cornell University. She serves on the advisory committee of [Film Forum](https://filmforum.org/), and lives in Baltimore.
